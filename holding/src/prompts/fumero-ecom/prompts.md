# Fumero Blog — Prompt Templates per Beeldtype

> Gebruik deze templates voor snelle generatie van blog-ready afbeeldingen voor Fumero.nl.  
> Vervang `[ONDERWERP]`, `[ZOEKWOORD]` en `[PRODUCT]` met de relevante bloginhoud.

---

## 1. Hero Image (1200×628 px)

**Gebruik:** Bovenaan blogartikel; tevens featured image in CMS.

```
Premium product photography of [PRODUCT/ONDERWERP], clean white studio background,
soft directional lighting, shallow depth of field, sharp product detail,
no text overlays, high-end minimalist style, Fumero.nl blog hero image,
aspect ratio 1200x628, JPEG.
```

**Voorbeeld:**
```
Premium product photography of a high-end espresso machine, clean white studio
background, soft directional lighting, shallow depth of field, sharp product detail,
no text overlays, high-end minimalist style, Fumero.nl blog hero image,
aspect ratio 1200x628, JPEG.
```

---

## 2. OG Image / Open Graph (1200×630 px)

**Gebruik:** Open Graph meta tag; preview bij social shares en in zoekmachines.

```
Clean, professional product image of [PRODUCT/ONDERWERP], neutral light background,
premium composition, no logos or text, optimized for social preview thumbnail,
aspect ratio 1200x630, high contrast, JPEG.
```

**Voorbeeld:**
```
Clean, professional product image of a premium coffee grinder, neutral light background,
premium composition, no logos or text, optimized for social preview thumbnail,
aspect ratio 1200x630, high contrast, JPEG.
```

---

## 3. Featured Image (800×450 px)

**Gebruik:** CMS-thumbnail op blog-overzichtspagina en in zoekmachine-snippets.

```
Styled product flatlay of [PRODUCT/ONDERWERP], [ZOEKWOORD] context,
premium minimal setting, natural light, clean background, no text,
blog thumbnail format, 800x450, JPEG.
```

**Voorbeeld:**
```
Styled product flatlay of espresso accessories, home barista context,
premium minimal setting, natural light, clean background, no text,
blog thumbnail format, 800x450, JPEG.
```

---

## 4. Inline Illustration (800×500 px)

**Gebruik:** Illustratie midden in artikel, bij een specifieke alinea of uitleg.

```
Detailed close-up illustration of [SPECIFIEK ONDERDEEL VAN PRODUCT/ONDERWERP],
white or light grey background, informative angle showing [DETAIL], no text,
editorial product photography style, 800x500, JPEG.
```

**Voorbeeld:**
```
Detailed close-up illustration of an espresso machine portafilter being tamped,
white background, informative angle showing the tamping technique, no text,
editorial product photography style, 800x500, JPEG.
```

---

## 5. Comparison / Guide Image (1000×600 px)

**Gebruik:** Vergelijkingstabel, top-X overzicht of guide-visual in artikel.

```
Side-by-side styled product comparison of [PRODUCT A] versus [PRODUCT B],
clean white studio background, both products clearly visible and in focus,
premium photography, no text or labels, guide illustration style,
aspect ratio 1000x600, JPEG.
```

**Voorbeeld:**
```
Side-by-side styled product comparison of a manual espresso machine versus
an automatic espresso machine, clean white studio background, both products
clearly visible and in focus, premium photography, no text or labels,
guide illustration style, aspect ratio 1000x600, JPEG.
```

---

## Tips voor snelle prompts

- Gebruik altijd het **hoofd-zoekwoord** van het blogartikel in de beschrijving.
- Specificeer **geen social media stijl** elementen (geen overlays, stickers, neon).
- Voeg altijd `no text overlays` toe om schone beelden te garanderen.
- Combineer met `batch_workflow.md` voor het in één keer genereren van alle beeldtypen per artikel.
